# Before HELLO! accepts real customers

- [x] Original Hello! raster logo supplied and integrated, with separate transparent word/exclamation layers. An official SVG remains an optional future sharpness upgrade.
- [ ] Approved hero, mobile crop, four category images, campaign banner and real product gallery photography.
- [ ] Actual catalog, LKR prices, garment descriptions, color/size SKUs, per-variant stock, care instructions and exact measurements.
- [ ] Approved brand story, core collection copy and any authentic brand claims.
- [ ] Official support email, phone/WhatsApp, social profiles and any publishable business address.
- [ ] Shipping zones, courier, delivery rates, COD eligibility and approved delivery estimate wording.
- [ ] Approved returns, exchanges, refunds, cancellation terms, privacy notice and terms of sale.
- [ ] Supabase project configured privately, migrations tested, RLS verified with separate customer/admin identities and production session/auth integration implemented.
- [ ] Owner-provisioned admin role; no public self-assignment of staff privileges.
- [ ] Merchant payment account and approved payment methods; secrets entered through environment configuration, never chat or source control.
- [ ] Verified payment callbacks, replay/idempotency tests, concurrent inventory tests and real order confirmation/fulfillment integration.
- [ ] Domain, hosting choice, business/tax details and launch approval.

The current preview is deliberately not published as a live commercial store. No real customer information or secret keys are needed to evaluate the design.
